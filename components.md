# Hardware Components

## Core parts

| Component | Qty | Example specification | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Piezoelectric disc | 4–8 | 27 mm common piezo disc | Mechanical energy conversion |
| Schottky diode | 4 | 1N5819 | Bridge rectifier |
| Electrolytic capacitor | 1 | 1000 µF, suitable voltage rating | Energy storage |
| Resistor | 1 | 100 kΩ | Voltage divider upper resistor |
| Resistor | 1 | 20 kΩ | Voltage divider lower resistor |
| Arduino Uno/Nano | 1 | 5 V logic board | Measurement |
| Breadboard | 1 | standard | Prototyping |
| Jumper wires | as needed | male/male etc. | Connections |
| Keyboard | 1 | test keyboard | Mechanical source |

## Optional improvements

- Low-leakage storage capacitor or supercapacitor for future experiments.
- Schottky bridge module.
- Oscilloscope for observing raw piezo pulses.
- Force sensor for correlation studies.
- 3D-printed or foam mechanical mounts.

## Cost strategy

Start with 4 piezo discs and a basic diode bridge. Expand only after obtaining measurable results.
